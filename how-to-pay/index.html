Reserve your invitation

We do not take card payments on this site. Once we have confirmed your design and your date, you pay by bank transfer or CliQ — whichever is easier for you.

  1. 1

    Message us

    Send us the design you picked and your event date on WhatsApp. We reply with availability and confirm the price.

  2. 2

    Transfer or CliQ

    We send you our CliQ alias and bank details. Pay the deposit, then send us the receipt screenshot.

  3. 3

    We start building

    We confirm receipt and start the same day. If anything is still missing from our chat — a photo, a spelling, a preference — we just ask for it there.
